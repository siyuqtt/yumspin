YumSpin Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026
← Back to App
Short version: YumSpin works entirely on your device and does not collect, store, track, or share any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

YumSpin does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or sensitive user data. There are no user accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics trackers, and no third-party advertising SDKs.

2. Local On-Device Storage

To remember your audio mute preferences (background music and sound effects on/off), YumSpin saves simple flags locally on your device using standard browser storage (localStorage / sessionStorage). This information never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Offline Operation & Network Requests

All meal ingredients, cooking instructions, illustrations, and synthesized cozy audio are bundled inside the app. When opened with an internet connection, optional typography fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts; the app falls back gracefully to system fonts when offline.

4. Children's Privacy

Because YumSpin collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

If YumSpin's privacy practices ever change (for example, if cloud sync is added in the future), this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
